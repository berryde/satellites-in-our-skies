## Overall Impression

The "mess of dots" is the core problem and it is not a UI-chrome problem. Above actual speed the dots are not moving along orbits: at "1 day per second" a low-orbit satellite does about 16 laps per second, so each frame lands it near-random. Paused and actual speed feel strongest because they are the only legible states, and they are legible because nothing moves. Biggest opportunity: a speed where satellites visibly go round, a dot field with hierarchy, and an opening that tells the story in the data (nine in ten objects arrived after 2019).

## What's Working

1. The moment-to-satellite jump: a timeline ring sets the date, pauses, selects the object, turns the camera and draws its orbit. The strongest flow in the product.
2. Copy and honesty: "One orbit takes 93 minutes", "Not launched yet on this date, so it isn't on the globe"; exact real-versus-illustrative statement.
3. Structural care: phone layout, slider semantics with spoken date, URL tracks paused moment and selection, Space and arrow keys, all 21 controls have accessible names.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] No speed at which a satellite visibly orbits
- What: speeds jump from "Actual speed" to "1 day per second" (src/state/store.ts:6-11). At 1x a low-orbit dot moves about 0.3px/s; at the next step it is resampled at near-random positions.
- Why: this is the flying mess; the product never shows objects going round the Earth.
- Fix: add "1 minute per second" and "1 hour per second", make one the resting state after the intro. At a day per second and faster, stop drawing per-dot motion and fade to a stable density or shell rendering.
- Suggested command: /impeccable shape

### [P1] The dot field has no hierarchy, focus or depth
- What: uniform 2.5px discs, equal brightness, colour by orbit class only. Opening camera crops the geostationary ring. Operating/inactive, owner, country unused visually.
- Why: the default view answers no newcomer question; the best image (fully zoomed out) is hidden behind an unmentioned gesture.
- Fix: additive blending at lower alpha; dim inactive objects; dim everything else to ~15% on focus; short trail on hovered/selected; one "lens" control with at most four options; mention zoom in the hint (src/App.tsx:137-139).
- Suggested command: /impeccable bolder

### [P1] The opening sweep has no narrative and no ending
- What: 20-second intro is linear in time; 16 seconds sparse, the Starlink era in about 2 seconds. Captions 12px inside the bottom panel. Ends on a frozen teal ball with a disabled "Today".
- Why: first and last impression; where "it doesn't seem very useful" lands.
- Fix: pace by events, hold at each of the five moments, 6-8 seconds on 2019 onward; large caption beside the globe; hide legend toggles and speed until it ends; finish with a pull-back, one sentence ("20,097 in orbit today. 9 in 10 arrived after 2019.") and two or three starting points.
- Suggested command: /impeccable onboard

### [P2] The controls misreport state
- What: speed menu says "1 year per second" at today (src/ui/TimeControls.tsx:61); headline count ignores legend toggles (src/ui/Legend.tsx:17); off legend row at 40% opacity is unreadable (Legend.tsx:41).
- Fix: show the effective rate at today; "showing 2,044 of 20,097" when classes are hidden; hollow dot with full-contrast text for an off row.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

### [P2] Camera dead ends and the timeline's empty 85%
- What: full zoom-in near-black (src/globe/SatelliteGlobe.tsx:38); no reset view; linear curve leaves 1957-2018 as a hairline (src/ui/Timeline.tsx:64); moment rings 28px and unlabelled on touch (Timeline.tsx:208); phone landscape cuts off legend and search.
- Fix: raise minimum distance; "Reset view" that appears only when the camera has moved; zoomable time track; permanent moment labels on desktop, swipeable chapter row on phones.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout
